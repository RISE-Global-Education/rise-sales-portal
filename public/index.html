How the numbers are calculated
  • A meeting counts only if it is recorded on Granola or the post-call sequence was sent through HubSpot.
  • Closed Won = deals in the Sales Pipeline's Closed Won stage.
  • Overdue Tasks = tasks overdue right now (not completed and past their due time in HubSpot). Pending, created and completed tasks are not shown.
  • Coloured chips use the sheet's targets: Portal 80%+, 20-min calls 50%+, average call 20+ min, conversion 30%+.
  • Each person's dates follow their own time zone (India or US Eastern).
